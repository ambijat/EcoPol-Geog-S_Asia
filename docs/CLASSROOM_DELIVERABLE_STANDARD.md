# Classroom Deliverable Standard

The primary end product of the IS529N repository is the classroom-ready lecture material from which the instructor teaches. Governance, source control, Git, validation, and the academic ledger support that product; they do not replace it.

## Canonical weekly product

Each numbered lecture ordinarily represents one teaching week:

```text
Lecture N
├── Part A — Tuesday, 9:00 am–11:00 am
└── Part B — Friday, 9:00 am–11:00 am
```

Part C historical files are supplementary exceptions. They do not redefine the canonical two-part Semester 2026 model.

For each part, the required deliverables are an editable deck, classroom-ready PDF, instructor teaching brief, source/provenance map, factual-verification record, and separate post-class record. The PDF lecture deck is the principal classroom artefact.

## Acceptance standard

A lecture part is not classroom-ready merely because a deck file exists. It must have:

1. sufficient content and planned interaction for a two-hour class;
2. a coherent analytical argument;
3. readable classroom typography;
4. readable maps, charts, and tables;
5. traceable sources and provenance;
6. current factual verification, including dates for statistics;
7. an explicit distinction between instructor material and AI-added material;
8. demonstrated continuity with the other weekly part;
9. an instructor teaching brief;
10. both an editable source and final PDF;
11. no unresolved high-risk factual claim;
12. explicit instructor approval.

## Part relationship

Part A normally establishes the problem, concepts, geography, regional patterns, comparative data, and analytical foundation. Part B deepens or applies that framework through cases, detailed evidence, policy, conflict, institutional or geopolitical implications, and weekly synthesis. Part B must not merely repeat Part A.

## Fidelity threshold

- `F0` — unverified draft
- `F1` — structurally faithful
- `F2` — source-checked
- `F3` — conceptually verified
- `F4` — instructor-approved
- `F5` — ledger-sealed

Only `F4 — Instructor-approved` material may be treated as ready for teaching. `F5` is reserved for material whose relevant academically consequential event has been sealed in the ledger by authorised action. Codex cannot approve or seal material.

## Classroom usability review

Review must occur in the final delivery format. A legible source deck can produce an illegible PDF; a correct chart can still fail at classroom projection distance; a dense slide can obstruct explanation even when factually accurate. The final PDF must therefore be checked independently for projection, navigation, sequencing, source visibility, and continuity with the teaching brief.
