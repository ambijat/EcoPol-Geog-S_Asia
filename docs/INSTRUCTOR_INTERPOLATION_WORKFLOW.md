# Instructor Interpolation Workflow

The instructor remains the academic authority throughout this process.

1. On Historical Slide 3, choose the academic action.
2. Write exactly what should be retained, checked, reduced, expanded or reorganised.
3. Record the decision.
4. Prepare the slide-specific corpus.
5. Copy the packet into a fresh external ChatGPT conversation.
6. Paste the returned YAML into the cockpit.
7. Validate it and correct any reported errors.
8. Compare the inherited slide with the proposal.
9. Open the editing view and make the instructor version.
10. Rebuild the slide manually in a protected working derivative.
11. Record that manual application and attach or reference the revised slide.
12. Resolve or explicitly defer outstanding claims.
13. Accept the reinforced slide or return it to the appropriate stage.

## Return targets

Choose the part that genuinely needs work:

- `SOURCE_CORPUS`
- `AI_PROPOSAL`
- `INSTRUCTOR_EDIT`
- `VISUAL`
- `CITATION`
- `SLIDE_SEQUENCE`

The returned proposal remains preserved. A later pasted proposal becomes a new revision rather than replacing the earlier one.

## Reading the status panel

The Slide 3 card reports the decision, corpus, copied packet, AI-result validation, instructor edit, manual application, attachment and final slide status separately. A completed earlier step never implies completion of a later academic decision.
