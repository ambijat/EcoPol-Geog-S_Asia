# Lecture 1A teacher incubation

## Governing distinction

The 65-slide inherited ODP is the canonical Lecture 1A pilot master and the
object of teacher incubation.

The 15-slide v0.5 artifact is a synoptic companion. It helps the instructor see
the whole argument, but it does not replace, reduce or become the revised
master.

## Incubation rule

Preserve the accepted master unchanged. Review it cluster by cluster. Use an
individual slide reference only to support cluster-level judgement or identify
a specific defect. Do not create a separate administrative ruling for every
slide. Create a working derivative only from explicit teacher decisions.

For each proposed cluster, record one preliminary treatment:

- `RETAIN`
- `REVISE`
- `MERGE`
- `REMOVE`
- `MOVE_TO_NOTES`

Then record:

- teaching purpose;
- evidence or citation needed;
- visual treatment;
- speaker cue and approximate time;
- whether the decision is provisional or confirmed.
