# Qt Phase 4 Triangulation

The **Lecture Triangulation** workspace is organised by academic topic rather than by file. It compares:

- `HISTORICAL_PPT_LAYER`: what was previously taught and how it was visualised;
- `RAW_RESOURCE_LAYER`: scholarly and empirical support, currency, and underused material;
- `WEBSITE_LEARNING_LAYER`: student-facing notes, explanations, and links.

The generic public-safe domain consists of triangulation topics, evidence, claims, findings, decisions, website-note metadata, source-coverage reconstruction, just-in-time registry candidates, and student learning packages.

Live records are local-only. The cockpit does not crawl websites, download attachments, infer AI origin without evidence, register canonical resources, approve learning packages, or publish.

## Analytical boundary

Retrospective reconstruction may combine lexical, conceptual, data, visual, and citation evidence. Lexical overlap alone never establishes probable source lineage. Instructor confirmation remains a separate field.

## Pilot state

The local Lecture 1A pilot extends the accepted “Meaning of a region” chain. Source-specific details remain in the ignored live database. Public tests use synthetic fixtures only.
